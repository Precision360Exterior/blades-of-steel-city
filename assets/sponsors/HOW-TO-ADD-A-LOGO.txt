ADDING A SPONSOR LOGO
=====================

1. Save the sponsor's logo file into this folder (assets/sponsors/).
   - PNG with a transparent background is best. SVG also works.
   - Aim for roughly 600px wide. Keep the file under 200 KB.
   - Use a simple filename, no spaces: acme-energy.png

2. Open index.html and find the section that starts with:
      <!-- ===================== SPONSOR LOGO WALL

3. Inside the correct tier group (The Cup, Crispy Sweaters, Biscuit
   Backer, Clean Sheet, or Icing), replace ONE of these lines:

      <a class="wall__open" href="#sponsors">Your Logo Here</a>

   ...with this, edited for the sponsor:

      <a href="https://acme-energy.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
        <img src="assets/sponsors/acme-energy.png" alt="Acme Energy">
      </a>

   If the sponsor has no website, drop the <a> and use:

      <div class="wall__tile">
        <img src="assets/sponsors/acme-energy.png" alt="Acme Energy">
      </div>

4. Re-zip index.html plus the assets folder and re-upload to Tiiny.Host
   using the Update button on your existing site.

Notes
-----
- Tiles are white, so dark logos and color logos both look clean.
- Logos scale automatically. Tall, wide, and square all fit.
- Tile sizes get smaller as the tier price drops. That is intentional.
- Leave the remaining "Your Logo Here" placeholders in place. They show
  how many spots are still open and link back to the sponsorship tiers.
- Once a tier sells out, that group will have no placeholders left.
