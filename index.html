Who We Are

Built by the Patch. For the Neighborhood.

Blades of Steel City brings together operators, midstream, service companies, and suppliers for one thing you can't get in a boardroom — a shift on the ice with the people you do business with every day.

Every team entry, every sponsorship, and every dollar raised at the rink goes to work in the communities where our industry lives: the towns we drill in, drive through, hire from, and call home. It's a half day of hockey with a full year of impact behind it.

Compete

Round-robin play across two sheets. Beer-league rules, real trophies, and bragging rights that last until next season.

Connect

Locker rooms and a post-game reception put customers, vendors, and crews shoulder-to-shoulder — no badge scanners required.

Contribute

Net proceeds are presented on ice to local nonprofit partners selected in each host community.

Game Day

Schedule of Events — Nov 12, 2026

Doors to final horn in a single half day. Times are subject to final ice allocation and team count.

10:30 AM
Check-In & Locker Room Assignments
Team registration table opens. Jerseys distributed, waivers collected, coffee on.
11:15 AM
Captains & Officials Meeting
Format, rules, and safety briefing with tournament officials.
11:45 AM
Opening Remarks & Ceremonial Puck Drop
Welcome from Blades of Steel City and this year's nonprofit partners.
12:00 PM
Round Robin Play Begins
Games run simultaneously on both sheets. Running clock, timed periods.
2:30 PM
Consolation Game
Third-place matchup on the main sheet.
3:15 PM
Championship Game
Top two seeds play for the Blades of Steel City Cup.
4:15 PM
Awards & Charity Check Presentation
Trophy presentation, MVP awards, and the on-ice donation to our nonprofit partners.
4:45 PM
Post-Game Reception
Food, drinks, raffle, and networking with players, sponsors, and guests.
6:30 PM
Event Concludes
